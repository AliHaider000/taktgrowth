Forms from search up 40% in 90 days, and a new channel from AI assistants.

IndustryB2B IT consulting and training, ServiceNow partner
WhereSofia, selling across the EU
Period19 Jun to 16 Sep 2026 against the 90 days before
ServicesSEO, AI search, Google Business Profile, tracking

Where they started

In May, AI assistants mentioned ITCE three or four times in total, and the organic baseline recorded 7 AI assistant users with no key events. Search Console was disconnected from the sitemap, and lead actions were not tracked the same way on the English and Bulgarian sites.

What I did, station by station

  1. Station 1 · MeasureSearch Console reconnected to the sitemap, and lead actions tracked on both the English and Bulgarian sites.
  2. Station 2 · DiagnoseCritical technical fixes, and the ServiceNow pages consolidated into one hub at /servicenow-consulting/.
  3. Station 3 · Build849 page titles and 531 descriptions rewritten. 652 vague links rewritten and 214 new links pointing to the priority pages.
  4. Station 4 · DistributeAI crawlers allowed, clearer page structure, Google Business Profile rebuilt, and 91 more websites linking to ITCE.
  5. Station 5 · Inspect90 days against 90 days in GA4, Organic Search plus AI Assistant only, with the summer slowdown working against us.
