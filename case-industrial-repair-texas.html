Industrial repair · Texas · name withheld

From 234 leads a year to 800, with nearly seven times the clicks from Google.

IndustryIndustrial gearbox and pump repair
WhereTexas, United States
Period2022 against 2021
ServicesTechnical SEO, content, link building, Google Business Profile, conversion tracking

Where they started

A gearbox and pump repair specialist with decades of shop-floor expertise and a website that did not show it. Engineers and plant managers were searching for exactly what the company does (gearbox overhaul, gear hobbing, pump repair) and finding someone else.

The technical base was slow: the server took 1.00s to respond and an average page took 3.46s to load. The site needed pages that answer the specific problems and part names industrial buyers search for, and tracking that counts every request for a quote.

What I did, station by station

  1. Station 1 · MeasureGoals set up in Google Analytics and Tag Manager, so every quote request was counted as a lead.
  2. Station 2 · DiagnoseSearch research into how engineers and plant managers phrase repair jobs, and a technical audit: server response 1.00s, page download 0.45s, average page load 3.46s.
  3. Station 3 · BuildService pages tuned for gearbox repair, overhaul, exchange and pump repair searches, plus brand and model pages for Falk, Western, Philadelphia Gear and Lufkin gearboxes. Server response cut to 0.38s and page download to 0.10s; Lighthouse 100 SEO, 100 best practices, 90 accessibility.
  4. Station 4 · DistributeProblem-first blog guides such as What are centrifugal pumps used for, which became the #2 landing page after the homepage. Link building for authority, and the Google Business Profile tuned for local repair searches.
  5. Station 5 · InspectGoal completions 234 to 800, clicks from Google 260 to 1,800, goal conversion rate 15.6% to 24.5%, and 29 keywords in Google's top 10.
Same company, same expertise: once the website answered what engineers search for, leads went from 234 to 800 in a year.

What to read carefully

Bounce rate rose slightly, from 59.7% to 63.3%, and time per session dipped, as many first-time readers arrived through the new guides. Leads still more than tripled. Two things to read carefully: leads here are goal completions in Google Analytics, not closed repair jobs, and the average page load comes from a 220-pageview sample.
