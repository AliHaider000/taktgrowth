Beverage destruction · United States · name withheld

9 keywords in Google's top 10 within a month of launch, without a single backlink.

IndustryBeverage destruction, alcohol disposal and ethanol recycling
WhereUnited States
PeriodDecember 2022 to January 2023, from launch
ServicesKeyword mapping, on-page and technical SEO, page speed, content, analytics

Where they started

A US company that destroys and recycles alcoholic beverages was launching a new website. Specialist B2B services like this are hard to find online, and new sites usually wait months before search sends anyone.

In the month before launch the site had 12 visitors, and every search I went on to track was unranked on 1 December 2022. A narrow niche is also the fastest road to the top, if you plan for it before the first page is written.

What I did, station by station

  1. Station 1 · MeasureGoogle Analytics, conversion tracking and Search Console live from launch day, so the first weeks were measured, not guessed.
  2. Station 2 · DiagnoseKeyword research across the niche: beverage destruction, alcohol disposal, ethanol recycling. Every page mapped to one primary search before design and copy started: commercial pages for buyers, blog posts for people still researching.
  3. Station 3 · BuildComplete on-page optimisation at launch (titles, headings, structure) and a technical setup so new pages were found and indexed quickly. Desktop Lighthouse 98 performance, 95 accessibility, 100 best practices; first content in 0.7s, largest content in 0.9s, 0ms blocking time.
  4. Station 4 · DistributeContent written for the questions buyers ask, such as a guide on how to dispose of alcohol. No link building at all: 0 backlinks built.
  5. Station 5 · Inspect9 keywords in Google's top 10 in the first month, and 4 pages in the top 10 by 18 January. In about six weeks, 4,260 impressions and 78 clicks from zero, and ranking keywords grew from 3 to about 50.
A brand new site reached Google's first page within weeks, because every page was planned around one real search before a word was written.

What to read carefully

No backlinks were built, so this is what planning, on-page work and speed did on their own. Read the volumes in proportion: 78 clicks and 4,260 impressions in about six weeks, in a narrow niche, with an average position of 27.7 across all queries. The figures cover the first weeks after launch and do not include enquiry counts.
