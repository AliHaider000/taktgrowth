Case study · ITCE · Sofia Tech Park

Twice the Google Ads leads at half the cost, 40% more forms from search, and a new channel from AI assistants.

IndustryB2B IT consulting and training, ServiceNow partner
WhereSofia, selling across the EU
ServicesSEO, AI search, Google Ads, tracking
Part 1 · SEO and AI search

Forms from search up 40% in 90 days, and a new channel from AI assistants.

Period19 Jun to 16 Sep 2026 against the 90 days before
ServicesSEO, AI search, Google Business Profile, tracking

Where they started

In May, AI assistants mentioned ITCE three or four times in total, and the organic baseline recorded 7 AI assistant users with no key events. Search Console was disconnected from the sitemap, and lead actions were not tracked the same way on the English and Bulgarian sites.

What I did, station by station

  1. Station 1 · MeasureSearch Console reconnected to the sitemap, and lead actions tracked on both the English and Bulgarian sites.
  2. Station 2 · DiagnoseCritical technical fixes, and the ServiceNow pages consolidated into one hub at /servicenow-consulting/.
  3. Station 3 · Build849 page titles and 531 descriptions rewritten. 652 vague links rewritten and 214 new links pointing to the priority pages.
  4. Station 4 · DistributeAI crawlers allowed, clearer page structure, Google Business Profile rebuilt, and 91 more websites linking to ITCE.
  5. Station 5 · Inspect90 days against 90 days in GA4, Organic Search plus AI Assistant only, with the summer slowdown working against us.
ITCE: the homepage alone produced 160 key events from search in the quarter, up from 109. Confirmed leads dipped from 87 to 76 while forms sent rose, which is the next phase's first job.
Part 2 · Google Ads

Google Ads leads from 20 to 42 in 60 days, with the cost per lead halved.

Period19 Jul to 16 Sep 2026 against 20 May to 18 Jul 2026
Budget€592 ad spend before and €636 after, paid by ITCE directly to Google; main campaign about €13 a day
MarketsBulgaria only before; then Bulgaria plus 35 European countries, ads in English

Where they started

ITCE's Google Ads account had run for eight months with nobody steering it. Before touching anything I audited its full history, October 2025 to June 2026, in a 60-page diagnostic. In those eight months nobody had added a negative keyword, reviewed a search term, changed a bid or tested an ad.

The account still produced leads, and wasted half its budget doing it: 49.5% of search-term spend went to irrelevant queries. Google picked most of the landing pages, Display banners took budget, and every click came from Bulgaria, although ITCE wants to grow across the EU and Central and Eastern Europe.

What I did, station by station

  1. Station 1 · MeasureConversion goals rebuilt so that only real leads steer bidding. Every result below is a tracked conversion, compared 60 days against 60 days.
  2. Station 2 · DiagnoseA 60-page diagnostic of the account's full history, ending in a 17-point plan: 12 points done in full, 4 in part (two wait on budget), 1 dropped as no longer needed.
  3. Station 3 · Build520 irrelevant search terms blocked on 11 dates, 521 keywords tightened from Broad to Phrase match, 75 removed. 5 themed ad groups (ServiceNow Consulting, ServiceNow Training and Talent, ITIL, AI Training and Consulting, Agile/SAFe), 255 new keywords, 6 new ads with 15 headlines each, and one chosen landing page for every ad group.
  4. Station 4 · Distribute35 European countries added to Bulgaria, in English. Ads Monday to Friday, 07:00 to 19:00, when leads actually came in. Target cost per lead lowered from €32 to €15, mobile bids +20%. A “Top ServiceNow Partner” lead form, 51 brand-compliant images and new sitelinks (AI Solutions, Training That Works).
  5. Station 5 · Inspect237 changes in 90 days, 96% of them mine. Leads 20 to 42, cost per lead €29.59 to €15.13. The gain held: 21.02 leads at €15.10 in the first 30 days after the relaunch, and about 21 at €15.15 in the next 30.
On almost the same spend, ITCE's Google Ads went from 20 leads to 42 in 60 days, and each lead cost about half as much.

Next steps

Raise the budget in steps. The main campaign runs on about €13 a day and Google reports it as limited by budget, so the next move is 15 to 20% at a time towards €30 to €50, while cost per lead stays under €20. Thursdays already bring 36% of leads, at €6.72 each on 17% of spend, which makes a Thursday bid boost the obvious next step. Fix mobile. On the website, desktop key events rose from 305 to 460 while mobile fell from 89 to 46; unblocking the page speed work (481 heavy images) is the next technical win. Turn forms into confirmed leads. Form submissions rose from 303 to 423 while confirmed leads dipped from 87 to 76, so a form audit and a monthly CRM reconciliation come next. Grow AI citations. AI engines already cite 48 itce.com pages but rarely name ITCE in the answer; schema markup, a curated llms.txt and third-party mentions follow.
